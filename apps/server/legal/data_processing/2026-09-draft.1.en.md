# Personal Data Processing Policy and Consent

> **Working draft** pending legal review.

## 1. Consent
By ticking the box you give your consent to the processing of the personal data listed below for the purposes below. Consent is recorded with the document version, time and IP address.

## 2. Data
First name, last name, date of birth, country, language, phone number, VOIDEX Mail address, device and session information, IP addresses, content of VOIDEX Mail.

## 3. Purposes
- registration and identification in VOIDEX;
- phone number verification;
- providing the workspace, Settings and Mail on all your devices;
- security: protection against unauthorised access and abuse.

## 4. Operations
Collection, recording, organisation, storage, updating, use, transfer to processors (SMS gateway), blocking, deletion and destruction — with or without automation.

## 5. Period and withdrawal
Consent is valid until the purposes are achieved or until you withdraw it. Withdrawing consent requires closing the account, because the data is necessary to provide the service.
