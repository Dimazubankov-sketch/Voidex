# VOIDEX Terms of Use

> **Working draft.** This document describes how VOIDEX works today and will be replaced by a legally reviewed version before public launch. Material changes will require your renewed consent.

## 1. What VOIDEX is
VOIDEX is a personal digital space: one VOIDEX Account gives you a workspace, system apps (Settings, Mail) and your data on every supported device.

## 2. Your account
- You must provide accurate information and a phone number you control.
- You are responsible for keeping your password secret. VOIDEX will never ask you for it.
- One person — one account. Accounts may not be sold or transferred.

## 3. Acceptable use
You agree not to:
- send spam, fraud, threats or unlawful content through VOIDEX Mail;
- try to access other people's accounts or data;
- interfere with the service, probe it for vulnerabilities without permission, or bypass rate limits;
- install or run software inside VOIDEX from sources other than the official VOIDEX channels.

## 4. VOIDEX Mail
At this stage VOIDEX Mail is an **internal** service: messages are delivered only between VOIDEX accounts. It does not send or receive email from the public internet.

## 5. Availability and changes
We work to keep VOIDEX available but cannot guarantee uninterrupted service during development. Features may change; we will tell you about significant changes in advance.

## 6. Suspension and termination
We may restrict accounts that break these terms or endanger other users. You may stop using VOIDEX at any time.

## 7. Liability
To the extent permitted by law, VOIDEX is provided "as is". Nothing here limits rights you have under consumer protection law.
