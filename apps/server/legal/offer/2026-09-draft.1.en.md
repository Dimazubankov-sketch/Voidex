# VOIDEX User Agreement (Offer)

> **Working draft** pending legal review.

## 1. Parties and subject
This agreement is concluded between the operator of VOIDEX ("we") and you, the account holder. We provide access to the VOIDEX workspace and system apps; you use them in line with the Terms of Use.

## 2. Acceptance
You accept this agreement by ticking the corresponding box when creating your account. Acceptance is recorded together with the document version and time.

## 3. Price
Access to VOIDEX at this stage is free of charge. Paid features, if introduced, will be offered under a separate agreement and never enabled without your explicit action.

## 4. Our obligations
- Provide access to your account from any supported device.
- Store your account data on our servers and protect it with reasonable technical measures.
- Notify you about material changes to this agreement.

## 5. Your obligations
- Follow the Terms of Use.
- Keep your credentials secret and tell us about any unauthorised access.

## 6. Term
The agreement is valid until your account is closed by you or by us under the Terms of Use.

## 7. Governing law
To be defined in the reviewed version according to the operator's jurisdiction.
