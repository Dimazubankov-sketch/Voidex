# VOIDEX Privacy Policy

> **Working draft** pending legal review.

## 1. What we collect
- **Account data:** first and last name, date of birth, country, language, phone number, VOIDEX Mail address.
- **Security data:** a salted, memory-hard hash of your password (never the password itself), active sessions, devices (name, platform), IP addresses of sign-ins, security events.
- **Content:** messages you write, send and receive in VOIDEX Mail, drafts, settings.
- **Consents:** which documents you accepted, their versions and time.

## 2. Why
To create and secure your account, sync your space across devices, deliver mail between VOIDEX users, prevent abuse (rate limits, brute-force protection) and meet legal obligations.

## 3. Where it lives
Your data is stored on VOIDEX servers — the single source of truth for all your devices. Devices keep only what is needed to stay signed in.

## 4. Sharing
We do not sell your data. We share it only with processors needed to run the service (for example, the SMS gateway that delivers verification codes receives your phone number and the code) or when required by law.

## 5. Your choices and rights
In Settings → Privacy you can see which documents you accepted and download a copy of your data. You can sign out of any device in Settings → Security.

## 6. Retention
Account data is kept while your account exists. Security logs are kept for a limited period to protect you.
